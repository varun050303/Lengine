KEY STRUCTURAL PROPERTY:
The target application evaluates identity (who owns the session cookie) but fails to
evaluate provenance (what origin initiated the request). The browser's ambient
credential mechanism acts as a confused deputy, supplying the victim's authority to
an instruction drafted entirely by the untrusted origin.
